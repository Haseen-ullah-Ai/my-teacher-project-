# Northwind Retail Group — Customer Satisfaction Report

**Owner:** Customer Insights  
**Period:** Q3 2025  
**Survey scale:** 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied)

## Executive overview

Customer Insights reviews post-purchase feedback by region and channel to identify service opportunities. The sample `customer_feedback.csv` contains date, region, score, and channel only. It does not include a customer identifier, transaction value, invitation count, or refund case ID. Therefore it can show observed response-score patterns, but cannot establish response rate, unique-customer satisfaction, sales causality, or refund-policy compliance. The Q3 planning reference score is 4.2 out of 5. Interpret regional means with counts and channel mix before making comparisons.

The primary service goal is a mean score of at least 4.2, with no region below 4.0 for two consecutive monthly periods. A regional average below 4.0 triggers a qualitative review of comments, where available, and a named service recovery owner. Any region with fewer than 30 valid responses in a month is marked low-sample; leaders should not treat small differences as statistically reliable. Scores are not rounded until the displayed result, and invalid values outside 1 through 5 are excluded and counted.

## Regional readout and response

North's delivery experience should be examined alongside the expansion strategy's 94% on-time readiness threshold, but survey ratings do not prove delivery performance. South should compare store and online channel scores before changing staffing. East should monitor checkout and pickup friction in higher-rent urban locations. West should review product availability and supplier-related complaints. Each regional team should document one customer-visible action, an owner, and a follow-up date, then measure whether the same issue declines in the next comparable sample.

Customer Care should route safety, privacy, discrimination, or suspected fraud reports to the appropriate specialist immediately. Ordinary service concerns should receive an acknowledgement within one business day. A resolved case is not necessarily a satisfied customer; case closure and survey score are separate measures. Where a comment is quoted, remove personal information and retain the survey date and channel needed for verification.

## Analysis guidance

Report response counts, mean, median, score distribution, and channel mix. If multiple surveys from the same customer are possible, describe the result as response-level rather than customer-level. Do not fill missing scores with the regional mean. For trend analysis, use consistent quarter boundaries and disclose whether a channel was added or removed. Sample rows are operational examples, not a probability sample of all Northwind customers.

**Caveat:** a leadership slide labels 4.0 as the Q3 target, while the current planning reference is 4.2. The 4.0 value is the escalation floor, not the company target. An older survey guide also says a score of 3 is “satisfied”; the active scale treats 3 as neutral.

The target and escalation definitions are distinct so that managers can see both ambition and risk. The Q3 memo's score discussion uses 4.2 but does not provide a confidence interval or response rate. That missing evidence should be stated rather than implied. Customer Insights publishes a methodology note with each monthly release and preserves raw, access-controlled responses according to the retention schedule. This report is meant to guide service improvement, not evaluate individual employees or infer the reason for a sales change. A recommendation should be linked to an observed pattern and checked against operational sources such as delivery logs or case records before investment or policy changes are made. If survey coverage differs by region, compare within channel or describe the limitation explicitly.
