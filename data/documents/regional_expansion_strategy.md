# Northwind Retail Group — Regional Expansion Strategy

**Owner:** Strategy Office  
**Planning horizon:** FY2025–FY2027  
**Decision review:** October 2025

## Strategic intent

Northwind Retail Group will grow in its existing North, South, East, and West regions before committing to a fifth region. The strategy favors profitable, serviceable demand over store count. FY2025 regional revenue targets are maintained in `regional_targets.csv`; the approved Q3 targets are North $215,000, South $200,000, East $225,000, and West $195,000. These values are baseline targets, not a forecast and not a substitute for a store-level investment case.

Expansion proposals must include a three-year contribution margin forecast, a lease sensitivity at plus or minus 10%, a staffing plan, supplier capacity, and a customer access analysis. The Finance hurdle is a contribution margin of at least 32% by the end of the second full operating year. A proposal with an expected margin below 28% is rejected unless the COO and CFO jointly document a time-bound strategic exception. Opening costs are evaluated separately and must not be hidden in recurring cost assumptions.

## Regional priorities

North has the largest approved FY2025 target at $840,000. It has strong repeat demand in home organization and outdoor goods, but delivery coverage is uneven in smaller towns. The near-term priority is to improve availability and raise delivery reliability before adding more square footage. A pilot may proceed only if 90-day on-time delivery is at least 94% and the projected margin hurdle is met.

South's annual baseline is $780,000. Its customer base is growing fastest in value home goods, and the strategy permits up to two small-format pilots during FY2026 if staffing turnover remains below 18% for two consecutive quarters. Store leaders must show that local inventory will not create excess aging stock. Promotional volume alone is not evidence of durable demand.

East has an $880,000 FY2025 target, the highest of the four regions. High rent in central markets makes site selection unusually sensitive. The Strategy Office expects proposals to show rent below 11% of projected revenue and a positive contribution margin in the base and downside cases. West, with a $760,000 baseline, is focused on improving supplier delivery consistency and reducing avoidable return volume before adding locations.

## Gates, metrics, and governance

The investment committee uses four gates: verified demand, operational readiness, financial return, and risk clearance. Verified demand requires at least 12 months of comparable sales or a documented test market with 500 or more qualified transactions. Operational readiness requires two trained assistant managers, a signed replenishment plan, and supplier capacity for 1.25 times expected peak weekly units. Finance publishes a monthly scorecard that reports sales against target, returns, labor, and contribution margin separately.

The plan is reviewed in March and September. A new proposal needs approval from the regional vice president, Finance, Operations, and the Strategy Office; leases require the CEO's additional signature. Exceptions expire after six months and cannot roll forward automatically. When a target or strategy figure differs, the approved target CSV governs quarter attainment, while this strategy document governs the investment hurdle.

## Risks and qualifications

The customer satisfaction report shows a Q3 average score of 4.2 out of 5, but its survey channel mix is not identical across regions. Scores should not be compared without sample counts and channel notes. Supplier reviews also show that aggregate on-time performance can conceal late high-volume vendors. Pilot readiness therefore requires vendor-level evidence rather than a single company average.

**Caveat:** an archived expansion slide says East rent may be as high as 12% of revenue. The current strategy's 11% cap is the approved gate; 12% is an older scenario, not permission. A second draft describes West's delivery requirement as 92%, but the current pilot readiness threshold is 94%.

This strategy does not direct managers to open a site solely because a region has missed sales targets. A shortfall may reflect stock availability, returns timing, survey seasonality, or incomplete source files. Proposals must reconcile those operational causes before requesting capital. At the portfolio level, no more than four pilots may be active at one time, and at least one must be in a region with a completed supplier capacity review. Strategy maintains a decision log with assumptions, approvals, and stop conditions. If the base case depends on sustained promotional discounting above 15%, the proposal returns to Finance for an independent margin review before the committee votes.
