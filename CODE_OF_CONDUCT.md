# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in ResearchPilot to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and professional.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and differing technical opinions.
- Make space for others and use inclusive language.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive conduct or other behavior that makes participation unsafe may also be addressed.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the contact route listed in the repository hosting profile or project issue settings. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible, protect reporter confidentiality where practical, and may edit or remove content or restrict participation when warranted.

This project applies these principles to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and public project discussions.
